In brief: this website explains OrderBridge and lets you enquire or book a demo. Applying does not automatically enrol your restaurant in the pilot or subscribe you to a paid service.

Website information

Product information may change as OrderBridge develops. Available features depend on your restaurant's configuration, the product stage and the applicable plan. Ask the team to confirm any capability you rely on before starting.

These terms concern the public website. Restaurant onboarding, product use and any later commercial arrangement should be agreed separately.

Pilot applications

An application expresses interest in the pilot; it does not guarantee selection or immediate access. We review the restaurant's needs and agree on setup and timing before the pilot starts.

The current pilot runs for 14–21 days with no fee during that period. What happens afterwards is discussed at the review. Applying does not commit you to a paid service; any subsequent scope or commercial terms must be agreed separately.

Some features are still being refined, occasional issues may occur, and not every planned feature is available. See the pilot page for the current scope.

Demo bookings

Cal.com displays the available meeting times and booking details. Please provide accurate contact information and check the timezone shown before confirming.

If your plans change, use the cancellation or rescheduling options provided with your booking, or contact the team.

Third-party services

FormSubmit supports pilot enquiries and Cal.com supports demo bookings. Their services may have separate terms and privacy information. Read our website privacy notice for an explanation of the current enquiry flows.
