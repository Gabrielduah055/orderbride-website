Privacy

How this website handles your information.

This notice describes the current public website, pilot enquiry and demo booking flows. It does not claim certifications or legal conclusions that have not been verified.

Information you choose to submit

The pilot form asks for a business name, contact name, email address, WhatsApp number, business type and a short description of your goals. OrderBridge uses that information to review and respond to your enquiry.

Demo bookings

The demo page embeds Cal.com. Information entered into the calendar is handled by Cal.com for scheduling and meeting confirmation. Review Cal.com's own privacy information before submitting if you need more detail about that service.

Campaign parameters

The site may capture standard UTM campaign parameters from the page address. These values help identify how a visitor reached the site. They are limited to the current browser session and passed with pilot or demo conversion paths. Do not put personal or sensitive information in UTM parameters.

Website storage

The site uses local storage for an explicit light or dark theme preference and session storage for UTM campaign values. It does not currently add third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

Questions and requests

Use the pilot enquiry form or demo booking page to contact the OrderBridge team about a privacy question. A dedicated public privacy email has not yet been configured.

Updates

This initial notice should be reviewed by the site owner and updated when product data handling, service providers or public contact details change.
