How the OrderBridge website handles pilot enquiries, demo bookings and information you choose to share with our team.

In brief: pilot forms use FormSubmit, bookings use Cal.com, and you can contact the team directly about your information. Please do not send customer order records or sensitive information through these enquiry forms.

What this notice covers

This notice covers the public OrderBridge website, enquiries and demo scheduling. It is not a complete description of how restaurant orders, customer conversations or receipts are handled inside the OrderBridge product. Ask us about those arrangements before connecting your restaurant.

Pilot enquiries

The application form asks for your business name, contact name, email address, WhatsApp number, business type and what you would like to try. It also asks for permission to contact you about the application.

When you submit, the form sends these details through FormSubmit for delivery to the OrderBridge team's email inbox. We use them to review your application, discuss whether the pilot fits your business and respond to your questions. Submitting the application is not a newsletter subscription.

Demo bookings

The booking page uses Cal.com for available times and scheduling. The calendar connects to Cal.com when you choose to load it; you can also open the booking page directly on Cal.com.

Information you enter there is handled through that service for scheduling and meeting communication. The booking host receives the details needed to organise the meeting.

Website storage and campaign links

When a link contains UTM campaign parameters, the website saves those values in session storage and may pass them with a pilot enquiry or booking link. This helps identify which campaign brought an enquiry to the site. Do not put personal information in campaign parameters.

The site's theme functionality can store an explicit colour preference in local storage. You can clear website storage through your browser settings.

The website code does not include an advertising or analytics tracker. Third-party services and hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as an IP address or browser details when your browser connects to them.

Services involved

  • FormSubmit: receives and forwards pilot applications.
  • Cal.com: provides the booking calendar and scheduling flow.
  • Email: the team receives and responds to enquiries using its email service.
  • Hosting and Iconscout: your browser requests the website and its externally hosted icon files.

These services have their own data-handling terms. Contact us if you need more information before submitting an enquiry.
