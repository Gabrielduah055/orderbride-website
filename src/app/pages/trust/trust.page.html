Owner and manager permissions

Access follows the role, not the conversation.

The same restaurant can serve customers, managers and owners through WhatsApp — each seeing only what their role allows. Permissions are set in the backend, not derived from message content.

Consent-aware outreach

Follow-up only where consent is confirmed.

When a supported plan enables customer follow-up, OrderBridge checks the customer's consent state, opt-out status and campaign version before sending. Outreach that is no longer eligible is cancelled rather than sent.
