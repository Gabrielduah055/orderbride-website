Role-aware by design

The same WhatsApp number does not mean the same access.

OrderBridge identifies the sender’s role and exposes only the tools appropriate to that person.

RoleExamples of permitted work
CustomerMenus, recommendations, own order and order draft
ManagerOrders, availability, images, reports, reminders and campaigns
OwnerManager tools plus menu-item creation and price changes

The principle

Let the AI understand the conversation. Let the system control the consequence.
