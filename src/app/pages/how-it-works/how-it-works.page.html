How it works

From menu question to accepted order.

OrderBridge guides the conversation through complete details, customer confirmation and a separate restaurant decision.

The customer journey

Each step resolves one specific uncertainty before the order reaches the restaurant.

  1. Menu enquiry

    The customer asks what is available. OrderBridge reads the restaurant's stored menu, prices and availability.

  2. Order draft

    Selected items are added to a saved draft tied to the current customer and restaurant.

  3. Quantity clarification

    Every item needs an explicit positive quantity. Missing details are clarified before submission.

  4. Delivery choice

    The customer chooses pickup or delivery and supplies the required address information.

  5. Delivery fee

    The backend resolves a flat, zone-based or manually confirmed fee from restaurant configuration.

  6. Customer confirmation

    The customer confirms the completed draft. This submits the order for staff review.

The important distinction

Customer confirmed does not mean restaurant accepted.

After customer confirmation, the order waits for an authorised owner or manager. Staff can accept or reject it using the latest structured order details.

Acceptance starts the next set of actions.

Only after the restaurant decision can the system continue with accepted-order side effects.

Receipt

A PDF receipt is generated from saved order and restaurant records.

Status updates

Restaurant staff can move an accepted order through preparation, ready and completion states.

Feedback

Eligible completed orders can receive a post-order check-in and feedback follow-up.
