Order confirmation

A customer submission is not automatically a restaurant acceptance.

OrderBridge deliberately separates the two decisions. The customer confirms their details first. The restaurant then accepts or rejects the saved order, and the customer receives the result.

1

Customer confirms

Items, quantity, fulfilment and delivery details are checked.

2

Restaurant decides

An authorised owner or manager accepts or rejects the order.

3

Customer is updated

Confirmation, receipt and later status updates are sent through WhatsApp.
