The product

Conversation connected to restaurant action.

OrderBridge gives customers a familiar chat experience while turning each supported request into structured, permission-aware work.

Book a demo

Three layers. One clear outcome.

Each layer has a different job, which keeps natural language separate from trusted operational truth.

Customer conversation

Ask, choose, clarify and confirm.

The customer uses ordinary language to explore the menu, assemble an order and provide fulfilment details.

Restaurant conversation

Review, decide and update.

Authorised owners and managers receive structured context and perform the actions permitted for their role.

Structured backend actions

Validate, calculate and persist.

Trusted services re-read prices and configuration, enforce order transitions and trigger notifications or receipts from saved results.
