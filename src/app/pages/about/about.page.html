About OrderBridge

Built from the gap between a conversation and a confirmed order.

OrderBridge is a Ghana-first product focused on making everyday restaurant ordering clearer for customers and more workable for teams.

Mission

Turn customer conversations into clear, confirmed restaurant orders.

Purpose

Make conversational ordering practical for restaurants and effortless for customers.

Ghana first, because the behaviour is already here.

Restaurants and food vendors already build customer relationships through messaging. OrderBridge starts with that reality and adds accurate menu grounding, structured orders and controlled operations.

The goal is not to replace the relationship with another marketplace. It is to help each business serve customers more clearly through the channel they already use.
