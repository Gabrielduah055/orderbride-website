About OrderBridge

Built in Ghana for a world that buys through conversation.

Food businesses already sell on WhatsApp. OrderBridge gives the work behind those conversations the structure it needs to grow.

Our starting pointGhanaian food businesses

Why we exist

The conversation works. The process behind it needs structure.

Customers like WhatsApp because it is direct, familiar and flexible. They can ask questions in their own words instead of learning another ordering interface.

Restaurants benefit from that accessibility, but every message can create manual work: checking menus, clarifying quantities, calculating delivery and confirming orders.

OrderBridge connects the natural conversation to structured restaurant actions—without taking either side out of WhatsApp.

Our mission

Help WhatsApp-first businesses operate with the clarity and reliability usually reserved for dedicated commerce platforms.

Our starting point

Build closely with Ghanaian restaurants and food businesses whose customers already prefer conversation.

Our long-term vision

Create a broader conversational-commerce layer for businesses that sell and serve through messaging.
