About OrderBridge

Built around how
restaurants already sell.

OrderBridge is a Ghana-first product connecting familiar WhatsApp conversations to clearer restaurant orders. Customers keep the conversation. Your team gets the details it needs to act.

Why we're building it

The conversation works.
The handover needs help.

A customer asks about the menu, changes a quantity and sends a delivery address. In a busy chat, those details can be difficult to keep together.

OrderBridge brings the request into a structured order for your team to review. The goal is simple: less copying between conversations and clearer decisions from submission to fulfilment.

Starting in Ghana

Build with the businesses using it.

We are starting with food businesses in Ghana that already take orders through WhatsApp. The pilot gives restaurant teams a chance to test the workflow in daily service and tell us what needs to improve.

We are not building another marketplace between a restaurant and its customers. We are helping the restaurant work through the channel those customers already use.
